Weekly Pacing
Weekly Game Limit above is a hard ceiling. These two control the shape of the season — whether games are spread evenly or crammed into a few weeks. Raising the hard limit without these will produce 4-game weeks next to empty ones.
What the scheduler aims for each week, as opposed to the hard ceiling. Leave blank to work it out from the season length (total games ÷ usable weeks). Weeks with too few diamonds to host the whole league are excluded from that maths.
Target maximum days between a team's games. Placements that break up a longer layoff are strongly favoured, and anything still over the target is reported after the run. A soft target, not a hard cap — refusing outright would just lose games, since the filler isn't chronological. 14 = at most one empty week.
These two goals pull against each other: packing games in fills more of the calendar, spreading them out leaves some unplaced. Off maximises games but gives teams 4-game weeks beside empty ones; Strong gives the steadiest rhythm and places a handful fewer games.
Sunday Pods
Sundays are your most valuable inventory — they usually carry the most timeslots, which is exactly what 4-team doubleheader pods need. One pod uses two back-to-back timeslots on two diamonds and gives four teams two games each. Left unchecked, a single division would soak up every Sunday, so these two settings share them out.
Hard cap on how many pods (across all divisions) may run on any one Sunday. Each pod consumes 2 adjacent timeslots × 2 diamonds, so raise this only if your Sundays actually have that much room. Higher = more doubleheaders but busier Sundays; lower = Sunday capacity is saved for single games.
Sundays are already filled before weekdays by default, so this only bites when Sunday slots would otherwise go unused. If your Sundays are already fully booked, changing this will make no difference.
Keeps singles off Sundays so they can't break up a pod's back-to-back pair. Works cleanly only when a Sunday's slots divide by 4.
Comma-separated division order. Each Sunday is assigned to the next division in this cycle, and that division gets first claim on the Sunday's first pod; any leftover pod capacity is open to everyone. Rotating stops one division from monopolising Sundays. List the division that most needs Sundays first.
Divisions
Hover any column heading for an explanation. DH Only makes a division play nothing but doubleheader pods — the strongest way to force doubleheaders.
Inter-Division Play
Cross-division games need three things, all at once: the Inter box ticked on both divisions above, the pair enabled below, and a games-per-team value above zero. Ticking Inter on its own does nothing. A division set to DH Only cannot play cross-division games — it builds its own pod matchups.
