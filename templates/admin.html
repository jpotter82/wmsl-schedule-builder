Admin is granted here, not at signup. Before this page it was set only by being the first account to register, with no way to change it short of editing the database on the host.

Role and plan are separate. Admin controls access to this page and the generator log. Plan controls what the account may do. An admin on Free gets Free entitlements — deliberately, so the free experience can be tested without a second account.

Changing a plan here is the only way a plan moves. There is no self-service route, so a user cannot put themselves on Pro. Every change is recorded below.
