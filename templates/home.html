Configurability

Your league has its own rules.
Skeddy understands that.

Most schedulers assume every league looks the same. Yours doesn't. Skeddy is a scheduling engine you configure, not a template you have to live with.

Change a rule, re-run, and see what it costs — because in a tight season, every constraint you add takes something somewhere else. Skeddy shows you that trade-off instead of hiding it.
